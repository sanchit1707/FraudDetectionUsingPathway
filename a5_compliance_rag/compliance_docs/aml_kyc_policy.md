# AML & KYC Compliance Policy and Transaction Escalation Guidelines

## 1. Cash and High-Value Transaction Monitoring
All international or domestic transactions exceeding $10,000 within a single business day must undergo immediate enhanced due diligence (EDD). Structuring or smurfing attempts where multiple transactions just below $10,000 (e.g., $9,500, $9,800) occur within a 48-hour window must be immediately escalated for card freezing and Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) drafting.

## 2. Velocity Spikes and High-Risk Merchant Categories
If an account experiences a velocity spike exceeding 5 transactions within 1 hour at high-risk merchant categories (such as cryptocurrency exchanges, overseas wire transfers, or casino/gambling merchants), the gateway must lock the card immediately to prevent further unauthorized drain. 

## 3. Account Takeover (ATO) Indicators
When a transaction occurs from a new device or IP address associated with high-risk jurisdictions concurrently with password or PIN reset attempts, immediate freeze protocol must be initiated. The SAR narrative must document the device fingerprint discrepancy and location anomalies.
