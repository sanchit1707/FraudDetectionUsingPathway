# Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) Filing & Drafting Guidelines

## 1. Regulatory Requirements
Financial institutions are required under Title 31 of the Bank Secrecy Act (BSA) to file a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) within 30 calendar days after the date of initial detection of facts that may constitute a basis for filing a SAR. If no suspect was identified on the date of detection of the incident requiring the filing, the time period for filing is extended to 60 days.

## 2. Essential Narrative Components
Every SAR narrative written by A8 SAR Drafter must clearly structure five essential sections:
1. **Subject & Account Details**: Full identification of the customer, card ID, and account status (e.g., Frozen/Locked).
2. **Chronology of Events**: Exact timestamp, merchant name, transaction amount, and velocity metrics leading up to the freeze.
3. **Suspicious Activity Description**: Detailed explanation of why the activity is suspicious (e.g., structuring, rapid drain, impossible travel, or deviation from customer baseline).
4. **Regulatory Basis & Violations**: Specific compliance policies violated based on A5 Compliance RAG retrieval.
5. **Action Taken & Recommendations**: Confirmation that the card has been locked at the Gateway Exit Point, current funds frozen, and recommendation for ongoing law enforcement or compliance review.
